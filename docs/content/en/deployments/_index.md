## Add your deployment

Do you run reva? Add your organisation to [`docs/data/deployments.yaml`](https://github.com/cs3org/reva/edit/master/docs/data/deployments.yaml) with a pull request. It helps other people decide, and it tells the maintainers who to ask when they plan changes.
