Enhancement: SpacesAdmin, SharesAdmin and StorageAdmin

Typed Admin API services for spaces and their quotas, shares and links, and storage routes. They use the CS3 messages
and find the service and the instance that hold the data. The `space`, `share`, `link`, `route` and `user` commands of
the CLI use them.
